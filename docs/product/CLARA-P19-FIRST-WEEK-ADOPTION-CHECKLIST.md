# CLARA P19 First Week Adoption Checklist

First-week checklist:

- Day 1: owner/admin confirms provider auth and workspace membership.
- Day 1: agents run customer create/update and notes workflow.
- Day 2: viewers confirm read-only access.
- Day 2: operators test conversation-to-customer linking.
- Day 3: operators review lifecycle/status and owner assignment.
- Day 4: team reviews issue reporting and escalation workflow.
- Day 5: team reviews known limitations and support boundary.

Mock/demo mode is dev/test only. Internal team usage must use provider auth.
Real workspace membership is required.
